🚗 Car Lights

Interior + 4 doors · Star lights 1 & 2 · Underglow over Web Bluetooth

Why do I have to pick lights again? Chrome on Android can’t yet remember Bluetooth lights between visits. The feature that would allow it (getDevices) is still an unshipped experiment, and the old chrome://flags switch for it has been removed, so there’s no setting to change.
What works today: tap Connect all once per light. Each tap opens the chooser for the next light, filtered so the right one is at the top. Then keep this tab open. While it stays open, lights that drop out reconnect automatically, including when you switch back to the tab.

Before connecting, force-stop “Dynamic ambient lighting”, “LED LAMP” and “Colourful LED” (Settings → Apps → Force stop). Each controller accepts one connection at a time. Works in Chrome on Android, but not on iPhone or iPad.
